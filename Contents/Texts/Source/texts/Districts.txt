## MSG_TUT_DISTRICTS
Districts are towns with no enemies. Serafew opens at Chapter 6, Port Kiris at 10, Caer Pelyn at 13 and Jehanna Hall at 16. On the world map a green flag marks them. Stand on the node, open its menu and choose Enter District. Walk with the D-pad and press A next to someone to talk: some give gold or an item once. Leave through the gate, marked with gold arrows, or press L and choose Retreat.[X]
## MSG_TUT_FISHING_TITLE
Fishing[X]
## MSG_TUT_FISHING
A fish marks the fishing spot: stand on it and press A. Choose a bait with left and right, aim with up and down, and press A to cast. When ! shows, press A at once to hook the fish. Then tap A to keep the line's tension in the green until the fish is landed. Bigger fish pay more gold, and a golden fish bites only the golden lure. Each catch also earns tokens.[X]
## MSG_TUT_CROSSING_TITLE
River Crossing[X]
## MSG_TUT_CROSSING
A frog marks the River Crossing spot: stand on it and press A. Hop with the D-pad across the road and the rafts on the river, and fill all five docks. You have three lives and 30 seconds a life, and each round is faster. B stops and pays what you have earned: gold and tokens by your score.[X]
## MSG_TUT_TOWER_TITLE
Tower Stack[X]
## MSG_TUT_TOWER
A tower marks the Tower Stack spot: stand on it and press A. Press A to drop each sliding block onto the one below. Lined up is PERFECT!; less than half on it costs one of three lives. Ten perfects in a row start a Fever, when misses are free. B stops and pays out gold and tokens.[X]
## MSG_TUT_HEX_TITLE
Hex Cards[X]
## MSG_TUT_HEX
A card over someone's head means they play Hex. Talk to them to start a match. Build a hand of seven cards, then take turns placing them on the board. A card attacks the foe's cards it points at, and a card with no HP left changes sides. Win to take one of their cards and five tokens.[X]
## MSG_TUT_GUILD_TITLE
Guild Board[X]
## MSG_TUT_GUILD
A notice board marks each district's guild board: stand on it and press A. Turn its pages with L and R. Quests count what you have already done; claim a finished one with A for tokens. A red ! on the board means one is waiting. Spend tokens on prizes, from Vulneraries to skill scrolls.[X]
## MSG_TUT_PROMOTE_TITLE
Enemy Promotions[X]
## MSG_TUT_PROMOTE
An enemy that defeats one of your units promotes at once, gaining its new class's bonuses and 3 more in every stat. Its sprite flashes into the new class and a notice names it. The Promote On Kill row in Options turns this on or off.[X]
## MSG_TUT_STATUS_TITLE
Stat Screen[X]
## MSG_TUT_STATUS
Press R on a unit to see its stats, and left or right to turn the pages. The Growths page charts its growth rates in green and its stats against their caps in blue. The Gaiden Magic page lists its spells and their uses, and the Promotions page its classes to come.[X]
## MSG_TUT_THREAT_TITLE
Threat Arrows[X]
## MSG_TUT_THREAT
Turn Threat Arrows on in Options. While you choose where a unit moves, red arrows point from every enemy that could attack that tile in their next phase.[X]
