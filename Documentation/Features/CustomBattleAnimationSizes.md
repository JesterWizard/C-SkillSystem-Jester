# Custom battle animation sizes

Generated by `python3 Tools/banim/sizes.py`. Sizes are what each animation in
`Contents/BattleAnims/BattleAnims.event` takes in the ROM:

- **Uncompressed**: the raw parts: the sheets (8 KB each), the script, the right and left OAMs, the palettes and the modes.
- **Usual**: inserted as FEditor Advance and FEBuilderGBA do it: every part but the modes in the BIOS's LZ77 (a greedy compressor), both OAMs stored.
- **Now**: what the build inserts: Huffman-coded sheets, optimal-parse LZ77 for the rest, the left OAM left to AutoGenLeftOAM ([CustomBattleAnimations.md](CustomBattleAnimations.md#compression)).

"Saved" is how much smaller Now is. ALIGN padding (at most 3 bytes a part) is left out.
